How we work

Four principles behind every decision.

From which manuscripts we accept to how we design our covers.

I

We publish for the long shelf.

A book still read in twenty years beats one that sells ten thousand copies and is forgotten. We build catalogs, not campaigns.

II

We don't publish to fill a niche.

We publish because a work demands to exist. No quotas, only conviction.

III

The author is not a product.

We're a small press. We answer our own mail, and every author has a direct line to their publisher.

IV

Wisdom is not a trend.

Sacred Balance Library titles aren't wellness content. They're frameworks for living, rooted in traditions thousands of years old.
