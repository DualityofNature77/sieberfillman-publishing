Buying & downloads

Where can I buy your books?

Digital editions are sold directly through our Payhip store. Kindle editions are on Amazon. Every title in the catalog shows the formats available for it.

Should I buy direct or on Kindle?

Buying the digital edition direct gives you a file you keep and can read on any device, and more of your purchase supports the press and the author. Kindle is best if you read on a Kindle device or the Kindle app, or if you use Kindle Unlimited.

I bought a digital edition. Where is my download?

Payhip emails your download link to the address you used at checkout, right after purchase. Check your spam or promotions folder if you don't see it. If you still can't find it, contact us with the email you used and the title, and we'll send it again.

What format are the digital editions?

Digital editions are PDF files. They open on any computer, tablet, or phone with a PDF reader, and you can print them for personal use.

I bought the wrong title or volume. What do I do?

Contact us with your order email, the title you bought, and the one you meant to buy. We'll sort it out with you directly.

Do you offer bulk or group orders?

Yes, for study groups, practitioner cohorts, classrooms, and organizations. Tell us the title, the quantity, and how you'll use it.

Kindle

Are your books on Kindle Unlimited?

The Modern Day Book of Life is available on Kindle Unlimited. Check each title's Amazon page for its current status, since enrollment can change.

My Kindle purchase isn't showing up.

Kindle orders are handled by Amazon, so delivery problems go through Amazon's Kindle support. Make sure the device or app is signed in to the same Amazon account you used to buy, then sync it.

Do you sell print editions?

Our titles are currently published as digital and Kindle editions. To hear about print editions first, join our list.

Nile sign volumes

Which Nile volume is mine?

Enter your birth month and day in the Nile sign finder on TMBOL.org. It shows your sign and the volume written for it.

How long is each Nile volume?

Each volume in the Nile Tarot Calendar Series is a full year: 365 days of alignment practices, meditations, and teachings calibrated to one Nile sign.

Do I need all thirteen volumes?

No. Start with your own sign. Many readers add volumes for a partner, child, or friend.

My sign's volume isn't released yet.

Several volumes are still in production. Sign up on TMBOL.org with your sign selected and you'll get one email on the day it's released. In the meantime, start with The Modern Day Book of Life, which every volume builds on.

Kemetic Alignment Practitioner certification

What does the certification include?

Three volumes: Sacred Law Foundations (Level I), The Sacred Balance Alignment Deck (Level II), and the Sacred Healing Masters Practitioner Manual (Level III). The full syllabus is on the certification page.

How much does it cost?

$39.99 per volume, or $80 for all three in the complete bundle, a saving of $39.97.

How do I get the credential?

Once you've completed all three volumes, the Sacred Healing Masters Assessment is emailed to you. You have three attempts.

Is it a clinical or counseling license?

No. It certifies you in the Sacred Balance alignment methodology. It isn't a medical, psychological, or counseling license, and Level III sets out the Scope of Practice.

Rights & permissions

Can I quote from one of your books?

Short quotations for review, criticism, or teaching, with the title and author credited, are generally fine. For longer excerpts, workshop handouts, or any commercial use, request permission first.

Can I share my digital edition?

Your purchase is for personal use. Please don't share or re-post the file. If you want copies for a group, ask about bulk orders.

Translation, audio, and subsidiary rights

We welcome inquiries from publishers and producers. Contact us, choose "Rights & permissions," and include the title, the territory or format, and a little about your house.

Writers

Are you accepting submissions?

Yes. Read the submission guidelines first. They explain what we publish, what to send, and how we respond.

Do you charge authors to publish?

No. We're not a vanity press. We never charge reading fees or publishing fees.

Do I need an agent?

No. We read unagented submissions directly.
