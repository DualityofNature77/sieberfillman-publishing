What we stand on

Four commitments behind every book and movement.

I

Name the system.

What keeps people poor, silent or uncredited is built, not natural. You can't dismantle what you can't see, so we make it visible.

II

Keep the record.

Dated, specific, permanent. The person with the honest record is the hardest to erase, and the hardest to divide.

III

Refuse the division.

Every group that has been set against another shares more with it than with the systems that profit from the split.

IV

Build together.

Not rage but precision: knowledge, documentation, solidarity and strategy, put to work for people.

What anyone can do this week

  • Amplify and credit. When an idea is repeated by someone else, say whose it was.
  • Interrupt the interruption. "I'd like to hear her finish."
  • Share the room. Pass on invitations, introductions and stretch work.
  • Be a witness. Write down what you saw, with the date.
  • Talk about pay. In most U.S. workplaces, discussing your own pay is protected.
  • Refuse the split. When a story sets one group against another, ask who benefits.
